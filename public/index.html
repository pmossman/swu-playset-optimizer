How it works

  • On My cards, unchecked cards drop out of the goal and cards you own come off it: boxes and singles only have to cover the copies you're still missing. Pulls of unchecked cards, or of cards you already have, are extras: trade bait if they're rares or legendaries.
  • Each pack is modeled as 1 leader, 1 base, 9 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 rare-or-legendary and 1 foil of any rarity (all adjustable under Pack odds). Hyperspace copies that replace a normal card count toward your playset.
  • For every card and every box count, the app works out the exact odds of pulling 0, 1, 2… copies, and from that the expected number you'd still have to buy. Expected cost = boxes × box price + expected singles − expected resale of extras.
  • Trading: duplicate rares and legendaries (including spares you already own) are traded at the rate you set for rares and legendaries you're missing. The credit is capped at what you're missing, so lots of boxes means lots of trade bait but few holes left to fill. The simulation settles every trade exactly; the main curve uses a close approximation.
  • The shaded band is the middle 80% of 400 simulated box openings: what you'd plausibly spend, not just the average.
  • Prices are TCGPlayer's, via TCGCSV, which refreshes once a day. Brand-new sets move fast, so check TCGPlayer before you buy.
